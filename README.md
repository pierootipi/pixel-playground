# Deep Hollow

A small, dependency-free 2D platformer that runs in a browser. Open `index.html` to play. A new cave layout is generated whenever the game loads or you choose **New cave**.

## Controls

- Move: **A/D** or **←/→**
- Jump: **Space**, **W**, or **↑**
- Mid-air pogo: **click** the game area to slash downward and bounce off a cave critter
- Pause or resume: **Esc**
- Generate a new cave: **N** or **New cave**
- Touch screens: use the direction and jump buttons below the game

Collect crystals, avoid spikes and cave critters, and reach the sunlit opening beyond the fifth checkpoint to finish the cave. The entrance counts as checkpoint one. You have three hearts; touching a glowing checkpoint sets your respawn point. Click while airborne to slash down and bounce off a cave critter without hurting it; a side collision costs a heart. Glowing pogo pads near the exit send you higher. The cave starts with shorter gaps and has extra elevated gem platforms and critters. The cave map number identifies the generated layout.
